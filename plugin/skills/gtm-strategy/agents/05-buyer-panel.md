# Agent 5: Synthetic buyer panel (pretest)

**What this is.** A rehearsal, not research. The AI plays the people in the beachhead buying decision so the team walks into real discovery calls with sharper questions. Every result is **rung 0** on the evidence ladder, a hypothesis to test, never evidence.

**Read first:** `inputs/company-context.md`, `inputs/evidence-log.md`, `outputs/01-market.md` (beachhead, personas), `outputs/02-competitive.md` (alternatives, differentiation), `outputs/03-channel.md` (pilot terms), `references/evidence-ladder.md`, `references/synthetic-panel-method.md`.

**Write to:** `outputs/05-buyer-panel.md`

## Steps

### 1. Build the panel
Create 6–9 personas covering the buying roles in the beachhead: **user, budget holder, blocker** (IT, procurement, compliance or whoever can stop the deal), across 2–3 company types from the ICP. Ground each persona in **behaviour, not description**: what people in that role have actually done, quoted from `evidence-log.md` wherever it exists (past purchases, workarounds, complaints, objections). Mark each persona *grounded* (built on real entries) or *ungrounded* (built on desk research only).

### 2. Run the pretest
Show each persona the positioning statement, the core value claim and the proposed pilot offer. For each, elicit **free text first**, in the persona's voice:
- Their reaction, in two or three sentences
- What they would compare it against (their current alternative)
- Their top objection, and what would overcome it
- What they would need to see before agreeing to a pilot
- Who else would have to say yes

Then, **in a separate pass**, rate each free-text reaction on the fixed anchor scale in `references/synthetic-panel-method.md`. Never ask a persona for a number directly.

### 3. Report (segment level only)
- Objections ranked by how many roles raised them, grouped by role
- Which message variant drew the least resistance, if variants were tested (a ranking, not a score)
- Where user and budget holder disagree
- The pilot terms each role would push back on

**Do not report:** price points or willingness-to-pay figures, individual-persona predictions, differences between subgroups, or anything stated as what buyers *will* do.

### 4. Convert to discovery
For each top objection and disagreement, write a **past-behaviour interview question** for real buyers ("Tell me about the last time you…"), and say what answer would confirm or kill the hypothesis.

## Format
Under 5 pages. Open with this line: "Synthetic pretest. Rung 0: hypotheses for discovery calls, not market evidence." Then a 5-line summary: top three objections, the role most likely to block, and the single question most worth asking real buyers this week.
