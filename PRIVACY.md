# Privacy policy: gtm-strategy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

gtm-strategy is an open-source plugin of instructions for Claude. The author runs no server and no service for it, and receives, collects, stores and shares no data from anyone who uses it.

It reads the company and evidence files you point it at and writes its outputs (strategy notes, evidence logs) to files in your own workspace. Those files can contain names of buyers or interviewees you supply; they stay where you keep them.

Anything you send to Claude is handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, not by this plugin's author.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dougkeiller/gtm-strategy/issues.
