# Strategic questions: Fieldnote (fictional)

## Hypotheses to test
| ID | Hypothesis | Why leadership believes it | What would disprove it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Contractors with 10–100 technicians feel the pain most | Founder's own experience | Owners of that size say end-of-day notes are not a top-5 problem |
| H2 | Owners will pay per technician per month | Industry norm for field software | Owners only accept a flat fee or bundle |
| H3 | Warranty-claim drafting is the feature that justifies price | Claims are slow and often rejected | Pilot users never reach warranty work |

## Known tensions
- Technicians are the users but owners hold the budget.
- Integrate with existing field-service suites now, or stay standalone?
