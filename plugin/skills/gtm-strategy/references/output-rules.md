# Output rules for every agent

1. **Lead with the answer.** Open with the 5-line summary your brief specifies.
2. **Specific or silent.** No generic advice ("build relationships", "focus on value"). Every recommendation names who, what and by when.
3. **Grade evidence.** Customer evidence by rung (`evidence-ladder.md`); third-party data HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW. Put the grade in the table cell beside the claim.
4. **Cite.** Every number has a source and an as-of date, or is labelled as an assumption with its arithmetic shown.
5. **Today vs roadmap.** Label every capability *exists*, *in pilot* or *planned*. Strategy is built on what exists.
6. **Test, don't assume, the client's hypotheses.** Verdict for each: confirmed, partly confirmed, refuted, untested.
7. **Length.** Stay under your page cap. Tables over prose for comparisons.
8. **Search.** Use the web search available in your harness for anything that can change (prices, funding, competitors). If no search tool is available, say so at the top and mark all market facts unverified.
9. **Stay in your lane.** Do not repeat other agents' analysis; reference it.
